# Human Cadence, Syntax Variation & Anti-Cliché Rules

This reference provides concrete sentence templates, before-and-after rewrite transformations, and lexical substitution tables designed to produce organic human cadence and bypass automated detection systems.

---

## 1. Sentence Cadence Patterns (The Burstiness Formula)

To create organic burstiness, structure paragraphs around one of these three cadence sequences:

### Pattern A: The Punchy Anchor (Short -> Long -> Medium)
* **Sentence 1 (Short, 6–10 words):** State the problem bluntly.
  * *"Static perimeters no longer protect modern workstations."*
* **Sentence 2 (Long, 28–38 words):** Expand with evidence, technical mechanisms, and contrast.
  * *"Because credentials and one-time tokens authenticate the user exclusively during session initiation, an operating system remains completely oblivious when an attacker seizes control of an unattended terminal or executes commands over a hijacked connection."*
* **Sentence 3 (Medium, 16–22 words):** Synthesize the operational consequence or solution path.
  * *"Addressing this vulnerability requires continuous verification mechanisms that run silently in the background without imposing cognitive friction."*

### Pattern B: The Concessive Pivot (Complex Concessive -> Short Decisive -> Detailed Explanatory)
* **Sentence 1 (Complex Concessive, 25–35 words):**
  * *"Although deep learning architectures report high benchmark accuracy by extracting latent features from raw mouse streams, their computational overhead and opaque decision boundaries hinder practical deployment on edge devices."*
* **Sentence 2 (Short Decisive, 7–11 words):**
  * *"Security administrators cannot audit a black-box decision."*
* **Sentence 3 (Detailed Explanatory, 20–28 words):**
  * *"Closed-form geometric metrics overcome this limitation by linking every authentication decision directly to verifiable physical distortions in the user's trajectory."*

## 2. Comprehensive Anti-Cliché & Vocabulary Replacement Matrix

Detectors rely on Bayesian weighting of characteristic AI words. Strip these words entirely from all manuscripts:

| Banned AI Marker | Why It Triggers Detectors | Authentic Human Replacements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"delve into"** | Virtually exclusive to LLMs | *investigate, scrutinize, evaluate, assess, inspect* |
| **"pivotal role"** | Excessive over-indexing | *central function, critical constraint, key mechanism* |
| **"testament to"** | Melodramatic trope | *evidence of, demonstrates, substantiates, illustrates* |
| **"tapestry" / "interplay"** | Metaphorical cliché | *interaction, structural dependency, coupling* |
| **"it is important to note"** | Pointless filler | *notably, specifically, (or eliminate entirely)* |
| **"foster" / "fostering"** | Corporate/AI buzzword | *encourage, promote, generate, yield, facilitate* |
| **"underscores"** | Massive frequency in LLMs | *highlights, indicates, demonstrates, reveals* |
| **"rich nuances"** | Vague qualitative praise | *idiosyncratic variations, subtle discrepancies* |
| **"beacon" / "transformative"**| Unsubstantiated hype | *viable alternative, practical framework, baseline* |
| **"moreover" (at paragraph start)**| AI default transition | *In parallel, Consequently, Simultaneously, Equally* |

## 3. Rewriting Transformations (Before vs. After)

### Example 1: Problem Statement
* ❌ **AI Generated (Predictable, uniform sentence length, cliché transitions):**
  > *"Passwords play a crucial role in modern cybersecurity. However, they are increasingly vulnerable to sophisticated cyberattacks. Furthermore, once an attacker gains access, traditional systems fail to detect unauthorized activity. Therefore, continuous authentication has emerged as a transformative solution to foster robust security."*
* ✔️ **Humanized Academic (Bursty, high perplexity, precise technical vocabulary):**
  > *"Passwords fail the moment a session begins. While credential-based gates prevent unauthenticated entry, they offer zero protection against physical workstation takeovers, session hijacking, or rogue insider activity once access is granted. The operating system remains blind to who is actually sitting at the keyboard. To eliminate this blind spot, security architectures increasingly rely on behavioral biometrics—passively monitoring peripheral dynamics to confirm operator identity continuously."*

### Example 2: Literature Gap
* ❌ **AI Generated (Repetitive triplets, generic phrasing, low burstiness):**
  > *"Existing mouse dynamics approaches have made significant progress in recent years. Many researchers delve into deep learning techniques, which offer powerful, accurate, and robust results. Nonetheless, these models require large amounts of data and lack explainability, making them difficult to use in practice."*
* ✔️ **Humanized Academic (Asymmetric clauses, active voice, concrete critique):**
  > *"Current mouse dynamics frameworks face an uncomfortable trade-off. While convolutional and recurrent neural models achieve competitive Equal Error Rates on benchmark corpora, they do so at the expense of computational efficiency and forensic transparency. Their dense latent embeddings operate as black boxes. When a legitimate operator is falsely rejected, an administrator has no mathematical basis for identifying which physical movement anomaly caused the score drop."*

## 4. Final Anti-AI Polish Steps

Whenever reviewing a draft:
1. Scan for the word **"delve"** or **"pivotal"** and purge them immediately.
2. Check if consecutive sentences begin with the same grammatical structure (e.g., `The... The... The...`). If so, invert one sentence to start with a prepositional phrase or dependent clause.
3. Check the word count of each sentence in a paragraph. If all sentences have between 16 and 22 words, split one into two short sentences (8 words each) and combine two others into a complex compound sentence (32 words).
